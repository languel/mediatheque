About Mediathèque

A personal public archive of teaching media: early cinema, chronophotography, movement studies, procedural graphics, and synthetic signal.

Mediathèque curates historical and generative media for research and creative coding. Every entry carries verified provenance, public domain attribution, multi-resolution delivery derivatives, and machine-readable metadata.

Formats & Signals

Video (VP9 WebM), animated GIFs, high-res plates, multi-track audio, 3D glTF/GLB models, Gaussian splats (.spz), SVGs, and markdown texts.

Integrations

One-click ComfyUI GeneretiMediaStream canvas paste, Python urllib/ffmpeg snippets, and llms.txt for autonomous AI coding agents.
